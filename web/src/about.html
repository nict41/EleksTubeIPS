About

HA Tubes is firmware for IPSTube and EleksTube-style six-screen clocks.

Firmware version: ...

Features
  • Clock faces, weather and slide shows on the six tubes
  • Full-screen panels pushed from Home Assistant over HTTP
  • The Tube Display Home Assistant app, which renders screens from your Home Assistant states
  • LED backlights, screen saver, MQTT and Home Assistant integration
  • Firmware updates over Wi-Fi on clocks with two app slots
